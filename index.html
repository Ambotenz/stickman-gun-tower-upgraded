HOW TO SURVIVE

A / D Move horizontally

W / S Float up / down

Mouse Aim with the cursor

A / D / W / S Aim in the direction you move

Mouse scheme Left click fires your equipped weapon; right click fires a rocket; Space dashes. Pickups automatically equip; 1 / 2 / 3 can select weapons with ammo.

Keyboard scheme Hold J to fire your equipped weapon; K dashes; L fires a rocket. Pickups automatically equip.

Touch screen Left stick moves, right stick aims and automatically fires your equipped weapon. DASH sits beside aim; ROCKET launches a rocket.

Pickups Rifle: 15 shots. SMG: 30 fast, low-damage shots. Heavy Cannon: 3 slow, high-damage shots. Weapon pickups automatically equip.

Space Dash and briefly avoid damage; time it against incoming shots for a perfect dodge

Walls Press away from a wall to kick off and reposition

P / ESC Pause the action

R Restart the run

Rockets damage every enemy caught in the blast, and enemies can shoot from anywhere in the arena. Keep moving, collect medkits when your health is low, and save rockets for groups or bosses.
